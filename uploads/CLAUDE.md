## Scoring System — Whoop Style

### Recovery (0-100%)
HRV-heavy weighting (chosen over Bevel's sleep-friendly approach).

| Component | Weight | Green | Yellow | Red |
|-----------|--------|-------|--------|-----|
| HRV vs 30d baseline | 50% | >5% above | within +/-5% | >5% below |
| Resting HR vs 30d baseline | 25% | >3% below | within +/-3% | >3% above |
| Sleep quality | 15% | >7h, >90% eff | 6-7h or <90% eff | <6h or <80% eff |
| Respiratory rate | 10% | normal (12-20) | slightly elevated | significantly elevated |

- **67-100% (Green):** Recovered. Train hard.
- **34-66% (Yellow):** Moderate. Train smart, avoid max effort.
- **0-33% (Red):** Depleted. Active recovery or rest.

### Strain (0-21, logarithmic — Whoop scale, NOT Bevel 0-100%)
- **0-9:** Light (walking, desk work, recovery)
- **10-13:** Moderate (active day, light workout)
- **14-17:** High (solid training session)
- **18-21:** Overreaching (competition-level, hard to sustain)

### Sleep (0-100%)
- Sleep need baseline: ~7h 30m
- Penalize duration shortfall more than stage distribution
